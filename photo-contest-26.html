Welcome to Aaron's Annual Sensational Photo Contest 5 (2026)

SUBMISSION DUE DATE IS: December-ish (TBD)


First things first, Rules

  • As usual, one picture for each prompt.
  • Any camera is allowed, whatever you're comfortable with you can use. If it captures light and can be translated to a digital format, it counts
  • All submissions should be a picture you've taken from this year (From 1st of January 2026 to submission date)
  • Once you're satisfied with your picture submissions, please email them to me by, at the latest TBD in a zip file with each picture labeled as its corresponding number in the prompt list.
  • This should not have to be mentioned but for clarity: You have to physically take the picture. Absolutely no AI is allowed for any editing or generation of your submissions and any AI use will invalidate ALL of that participants submissions.
  • Any AI image generation for submissions or about the contest (Creating flyers for the prompts or rules) will also disqualify all of that participant's submissions. All the information for the contest is hosted here and if you'd like to modify it, put in the work yourself.
  • The only post-processing of your images that is allowed is cropping, all other image effects disqualify that submission.

Finally, the prompt list

  1. A picture with long lines (Any way you'd like to interpret it)
  2. A vibrant picture with intense color (Intense colors or many colors)
  3. A micro picture (Something very small)
  4. A picture of something that looks out of place
  5. A still life (Inanimate and commonplace objects arranged in a scene)
  6. A freespace (Anything you'd like)
  7. A picture of a sign (Store sign, informational sign, any infographic)
  8. A picture of plants (Any multicellular eukaryotic organism)
