Welcome to Aaron's Annual Remarkable Photo Contest 4 (2025)

First things first, Rules

  • As usual, one picture for each prompt.
  • Any camera is allowed, whatever you're comfortable with you can use. If it captures light and can be translated to a digital format, it counts
  • All submissions should be a picture you've taken from this year (From 1st of January 2025 to now)
  • Once you're satisfied with your picture submissions, please email them to me by, at the latest Dec. 20th in a zip file with each picture labeled as its corresponding number in the prompt list.
  • This should not have to be mentioned but for clarity: You have to physically take the picture. Absolutely no AI is allowed for any editing or generation of your submissions and any AI use will invalidate ALL of your submissions.
  • The only post-processing of your images that is allowed is cropping, all other image effects disqualify that submission.

Finally, the prompt list

  1. A picture of Food
  2. A picture of an Art Piece (Sculpture, Painting, Visual Piece)
  3. A picture of Plants (Flower, Tree, Fungus)
  4. A picture that's visually a majority PURPLE
  5. A picture that evokes a specific mood or emotion (Makes you happy, sad, etc)
  6. A picture with dramatic lighting (Any subject, focus on the lighting)
  7. An establishing shot (Very long or wide shot meant to establish a location or theme)
  8. A picture of something up close
